## Short story

High-dimensional data often hides its groups behind irrelevant dimensions and nonlinear structure. An autoencoder learns a compact representation, then deep clustering methods use that representation to make groups more coherent. The survey organizes methods by training strategy: sequential, alternating, and simultaneous approaches. The central data-mining question is not simply whether a neural network reconstructs the input. It is whether the learned space supports meaningful, stable, and useful clusters.
